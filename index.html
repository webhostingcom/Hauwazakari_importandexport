BUY WITH CONFIDENCE

Protect your investment.

Important things every car buyer and importer should understand before making an automotive purchase.

Before purchasing a vehicle, inspect its condition, history and documents carefully.

Review available vehicle history, import papers and ownership documents before completing the purchase.

Compare vehicle prices, specifications, condition and market value before making a decision.

Consider shipping, customs, clearing and other applicable costs when importing a vehicle.

Confirm the agreed vehicle price, payment stages and other transaction costs before making payments.

Before taking delivery, confirm the vehicle, documents and agreed handover requirements have been properly handled.
